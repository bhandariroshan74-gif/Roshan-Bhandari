About Me

Hi, I’m Roshan Bhandari, a tech enthusiast, creative designer, and passionate learner from Nepal. I am currently pursuing BSc CSIT and exploring the world of technology, design, and digital creativity. I have a strong interest in graphic design, UI/UX design, cybersecurity, frontend development, and AI & Machine Learning. Alongside my studies, I have been involved in leadership, volunteering, youth initiatives, and community activities. I believe in continuously learning, trying new things, and turning ideas into meaningful work. I created this website as my personal digital space to share my journey, projects, skills, experiences, and creative work. Tech enthusiast. Creative thinker. Passionate learner. Always exploring.
